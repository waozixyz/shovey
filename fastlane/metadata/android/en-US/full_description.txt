Push. Solve. Come home.

Shovey is a puzzle game about finding a path home. Push balls into matching pits, move crates onto their marks, collect what you need and untangle the board one move at a time.

Play the Classic campaign with swipe controls, an on-screen movement pad or a keyboard. Saved progress and a level chooser let you revisit unlocked puzzles. Portals, levers, gates, pressure buttons and wandering animals change the route.

Make your own boards in the integrated level editor. Paint the ground, objects and decorations, undo a stroke and play your puzzle with the same rules as the campaign. Drafts save as you edit.

Play locally without an account. Optional Profile and node services are available separately. No advertising or analytics SDK is included.

Shovey is free and open source, made by Waozi.
