Privacy and account data

Updated 23 October 2026. Shovey is made by Waozi.

Local puzzles and progress

You can play Shovey without an account. Progress, preferences and editor drafts are stored on your device. Shovey has no advertising or analytics SDK. Your device or browser may include app data in its own backup system; those copies are governed by that system's settings.

Optional profile and node services

Profile can connect an account to a Daochi node. Explicit account, device-link and node-service requests use the network. The selected node receives the account identifiers and service requests needed for those features; network services can observe connection information such as an IP address and request time. The default Waozi node is api.waozi.xyz. Other nodes have their own operators and policies. Local game progress is separate from these account services.

Delete account data

Profile's account-management screen offers remote-data clearing and logout. For help deleting data held by Waozi's node without reinstalling Shovey, email waozi@proton.me with the subject “Shovey account deletion”, your public account ID and the node address. We must verify control of the account before removing its data. Never send a private account key, password or sign-in code. For a different node, contact its operator.

Logging out or clearing remote account data does not erase local progress, exported levels, device backups or other devices' copies. Your operating system's app-data controls can remove local Android data; your browser's site-data controls can remove the browser game's local data. Back up anything you want to retain before clearing it.
