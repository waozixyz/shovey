Shovey Privacy Policy

Updated 27 October 2026. This policy covers the Shovey game for Android, desktop and the web, made by Waozi. Privacy contact: waozi@proton.me.

Playing on your device

You can play the included puzzles without creating an account. Shovey stores preferences, unlocked puzzles, completed levels, unfinished boards, editor drafts and locally earned reward status on your device or in your browser. A local completion history can include the puzzle version, moves and route used to solve it. Shovey includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Your device or browser may include app data in its own backup system. Those copies are governed by that system's settings. Exported levels and account backups you choose to save are also under your control.

Online features and connection information

Fetching campaign updates or shared levels, connecting an account and using node services require a network connection. The receiving service and its hosting providers can process connection information such as your IP address and request time to deliver the request and protect the service. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

The default Waozi node is https://api.waozi.xyz. Where the app lets you select another node, that node has its own operator and privacy practices. Selecting a node alone does not create an account or enable account synchronization.

Optional accounts, progress and rewards

When you connect an account, the account service uses your public account identifier and alias, if set, to provide account, device-connection and synchronization features. The native host manages account keys and recovery. Do not send private keys, passwords or recovery codes to support.

On hosts that support account synchronization, earned unlocks and completion marks can be synchronized with your connected account. Supported editor records can also be synchronized. The unfinished board and movement saves stay on the device. Synced private records can be encrypted by the host before upload; public identifiers and requests to use a service are still visible to its operator.

To verify completed puzzles and optional Dew rewards, the account service may submit completion evidence, including the game and level identifier, puzzle version, moves or solution route, and the account claiming the completion. The node uses this information to validate earned progress and prevent duplicate or invalid reward claims.

If you use the optional Dew services in Profile, the selected node processes your account identifier, balance, reward claims, funding-address requests and storage-plan purchases. Funding and payments are handled by the node and the applicable payment network. Local puzzles do not require a payment or a connected account.

In the Google Play version, Dew is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the Waozi node receives the Dew pack and Google's purchase token, confirms the purchase with Google and adds the Dew to your account. Waozi never receives your payment details. The direct download and F-Droid versions buy Dew with Monero at dew.waozi.xyz.

Sharing levels

If you choose to publish a level collection through a supported host, the selected node receives the collection, its title, puzzle data, winning routes, visibility setting and your public account identifier or alias. Public collections and their author details can be seen by other users. Choose the offered visibility setting before sharing; unpublishing does not remove copies that other people have already downloaded.

How data is used and shared

These services use data to deliver requested content, keep account records available across supported devices, verify progress and rewards, and operate and protect the service. Shovey does not send data to advertising or analytics SDKs. Node operators, hosting providers and any payment service you choose receive the information needed for the features they provide. Publishing a collection also shares it with the audience you select.

The default Waozi endpoints use HTTPS. The security and retention practices of another node depend on its operator. Account-key backups, device connection and exports should be handled with care because they can grant access to an account or contain its data.

Retention and deleting account data

Local saves remain until you remove them using the app or your operating system's app-data controls. Browser saves can be removed through the browser's site-data controls. Back up anything you want to keep before clearing data.

Profile's account-management screen offers remote-data clearing on supported hosts and logout. Synced content remains on the selected node until removed through those controls or its operator. Logging out alone does not delete remote data.

You can request deletion of your account and associated data held by Waozi's node without reinstalling Shovey. Email waozi@proton.me with the subject Shovey account deletion, your public account ID and the node address. We must verify that you control the account before acting. Never send a private account key, password or sign-in code. For a different node, contact that node's operator.

Remote-data clearing or an account-deletion request does not erase local saves, exported files, device backups or other people's copies of shared levels. If service, security or legal requirements prevent removal of particular records, the operator must explain what remains and why when handling the request.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions, account-data requests or questions about a child's data, contact waozi@proton.me. This page will be updated when Shovey's data handling changes. Its date shows the latest revision.
