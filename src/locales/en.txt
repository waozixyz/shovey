[shovey_instructions]
Use arrow keys or WASD. Push one object at a time; objects cannot be pulled. Put balls into matching colored holes and crates on their marked squares. Paint recolors a ball. Collect the apples and bring star blocks together. Finish the tasks before going home. Crates hold pressure doors open. Keys open locks. Space throws a collected projectile in the direction Shovey faces. Some pickups reverse controls, leave a trail or make Shovey slide until blocked. Charged Shovey can light a circuit; avoid charging twice. Cycling blocks change red → blue → yellow with each push; their goals need red. R restarts, and H opens this guide.
