## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-19

- Make your own levels for Shovey Classic and Shovey Island: paint any piece by touch or mouse, pick pieces up, undo a stroke at a time.
- Island shores and banks follow the sand, meadow and water you paint.
- Browse every story level; painting on one keeps your changes as your own copy and leaves the story as it is.
- A solver checks each level while you build it; winning it yourself also proves it.
- Play your levels with the game's controls; every change is kept as you make it.
- Copy your proven levels as text for Shovey's developer.
- Share proven levels as public, friends-only or private collections, and add others' collections to your levels, where the app you play in offers sharing.
